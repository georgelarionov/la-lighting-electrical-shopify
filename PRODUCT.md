# PRODUCT.md — Los Angeles Lighting & Electrical

**Register:** brand (marketing storefront homepage; the design IS the product).

**What it is:** A Shopify Hydrogen storefront for a Los Angeles commercial &
industrial lighting and electrical contractor + fixture catalog. Sells linear
and architectural lighting, and books electrical/lighting-design services.

**Audience:** Architects, contractors, facility managers, and design-minded
commercial buyers specifying lighting for offices, retail, and hospitality.

**Voice (three physical words):** precise · engineered · luminous.
Quiet confidence, spec-sheet credibility, gallery restraint. Apple-like, but
for light. No buzzwords, no em dashes, no aphoristic cadence.

**Aesthetic lane (named reference):** Apple.com restraint — photography-first
full-bleed tiles alternating light/dark, one quiet blue action color, SF Pro
typography with tight tracking, museum whitespace. NOT editorial-serif, NOT
SaaS-card-grid. Modeled on apple.com/uk proportions.

**Committed brand identity (preserve — do not re-pick):**
- Action Blue `#0066cc` — the single interactive/action color (links, CTAs, focus).
- Ink `#1d1d1f`, body on light. Parchment `#f5f5f7` (cool near-white, hue ~286 —
  not a warm-cream tell). Dark tile `#272729`. White `#ffffff`.
- Green `#22c55e` reserved strictly for the non-interactive "Licensed & Insured" mark.
- Font: SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text via `system-ui, -apple-system` stack.
- Radius: 2px everywhere; full-bleed tiles 0px. (Client override of DESIGN.md pills.)
- Full token system lives in `DESIGN.md` and `app/styles/tailwind.css`.

**Homepage sections:** header (slim utility bar + frosted nav), hero, catalog
slider, services, projects, request-a-quote, blog + newsletter, footer.

**Imagery:** real lighting/architectural photography (no gray placeholder blocks).
Content adapted and rewritten (not copied) from architectural-lighting references.

**Scope now:** desktop homepage, designed in the open `.pen` file via Pencil.
